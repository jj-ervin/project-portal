# ProPro — Product Charter v0.1

Status: proposed baseline for review. Date: 2026-11-04.

## Mission
One place to manage the work you do, however you work. ProPro is a universal project and portfolio management platform for individuals, freelancers, researchers, builders, creative professionals, businesses, and software teams.

## Non-negotiable principles
- Project types are composable capabilities, not exclusive categories.
- Construction and research are first-class, alongside freelance, software, creative, publication, business and personal projects.
- Simple single-project onboarding; scalable portfolios and teams.
- Core usable without GitHub, AI, Governance Commons or internet-only services.
- Portable data, transparent ownership, meaningful exports and offline-capable architecture assessment.
- Evidence and decisions retain provenance; uncertainty is explicit.
- Human approval for consequential automation; accessibility and visual acceptance criteria from inception.
- Integrate existing tools rather than duplicate their responsibilities.

## Shared concepts
Workspace, portfolio, project, relationships, participant, role, task, milestone, deliverable, resource, artifact, decision, evidence, activity and capability. Projects may belong to portfolios and link to other projects through typed, traceable relationships. A repository is a linked resource, not the definition of a project.

## Capability examples
- Construction: measurements, drawings, material takeoffs, procurement, contractors, permits, inspections, progress photos and costs.
- Research: questions, hypotheses, protocols, sources, datasets, experiments, findings, citations and publication.
- Freelance: clients, proposals, scopes, deliverables, reviews, approvals, expenses and invoice tracking.
- Software: Git repositories, issues, CI, releases and dependencies.
- Creative/publication: assets, drafts, revisions, editorial approvals and publishing.

## Roadmap
A — Portfolio visibility and universal project organization first.
B — Coordination, evidence, integrations and portfolio intelligence.
C — Integrated specialist workspaces and governed execution.
Design extension points for C, but do not build C into the MVP.

## MVP acceptance
Users can create projects without external accounts; organize portfolios; combine capability templates; manage tasks, milestones and deliverables; connect projects with typed relationships; find and navigate work in an accessible responsive UI; persist and export information. Validate end-to-end scenarios for construction, research and freelance client work.

## Scope guardrails
Do not build a full ERP, accounting package, laboratory information system, IDE, marketplace or payment processor in the MVP. No assumed dependencies on Cockpit, Marlin, DCP/DXP or Governance Commons until assessed.

## Product hypothesis
Adaptive project workspaces plus an explainable relationship graph and evidence-backed impact analysis could differentiate ProPro. This is a hypothesis requiring user and competitor validation, not an established market fact.
